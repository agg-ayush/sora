# Revenue proposal review bar (from infra lead's decisions)

- infra lead rejects bundled items: "a, b, c are separate and don't need to be in 1 item." One outcome per item; extra conditions only as guards ("This counts only if ...").
- Candidate/user counts as the claim read as "work done not value... is a milestone." Put volume in a guard, in the definition of which round counts ("the first round of at least 100 candidates who finished..."), or in a milestone, not the headline.
- Name whose time is saved and who confirms; best shape is confirmer = beneficiary (customer confirms their own time recovered). State today's baseline in one line ("Today she schedules every session herself").
- Scope the time claim to exactly the step the system removes. If the customer will still do an adjacent step (e.g. outreach), leave it out of the claim rather than over-promising.
- Define every fuzzy term: which round counts, what "completed" means, who concerns are reported to. When a size condition moves into the round definition, check the completion bar didn't fall out with it.
- Call out unknowns not yet agreed with the customer.
- Milestones must link to an approved revenue item, so they are created only after the investor approves the proposal. Milestones can be a named person confirming an event (a batch ran clean) or a dated outcome (system live, N users through it); write each as a complete statement, not a fragment.
- Fundability clocks may count only an approved plan, not a submitted one: ping the decider.
- Audit procedure that worked: ask the TiCoons agent to compare the draft against all approved and rejected proposals platform-wide and list shared traits with rejections plus gaps. Rejections with real reasoning are rare (most are author withdrawals), so the decider's own past rejection is the most predictive evidence.
