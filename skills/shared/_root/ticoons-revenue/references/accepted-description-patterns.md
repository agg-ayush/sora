# Patterns in strong Trilovation descriptions

Observed across the platform's ACTIVE and recently rewritten descriptions (e.g. ADD SelfServe, Finance Collections, Legal Intake V4, Alpha Coaching Dashboard, Edu Finance). Most PAUSED/ABANDONED entries are one-line taglines and are not the bar.

1. **First sentence says what the product is and does**, not the persona's day or the problem ("An AI email assistant for the finance collections team", "<Product> replaces the R0 and R1 rounds with an automated assessment"). Problem and status quo follow.
2. **Concrete scale numbers** ground the pain: headcount, volume per month, number of systems/files, hours spent. Use only numbers from a real source.
3. **Named real people with their role in the flow** (who owns, who approves, who does the manual step). Outsider-unknown roles get a short explanation.
4. **Precise about where things live**: which system runs the live piece, who copies what where. Avoid "this is live today" without naming the system.
5. **Current state stated exactly, gaps stated concretely**, plain flat sentences, no marketing.
6. **ACTIVE ones carry the quarter goal with a date** ("by <date>, hand over to HR so <person> can do X in N clicks"). A BOOTSTRAPPING description may omit it while the plan is undecided, but needs it before investor review.
7. Some long ones add a short "what X means" clarifier block for a term that bounds scope (e.g. what counts as a "simple step" the agent may take).
