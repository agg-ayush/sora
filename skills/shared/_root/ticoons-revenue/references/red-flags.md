# Red flags

Red flags are primarily risks to value delivery or revenue earning, that anybody has identified. They can also be anything out of place that someone notices.

# Objective

The objective behind recording a red flag is to:
- add a record in the Trilovation timeline that along with CEO/Investor decisions and events gives us insight into how the Trilovation ran so we can learn from our mistakes.
- trigger action
- record other peoples opinions
- record if and when the red flag is resolved.

# What they are not

The are NOT escalations, or complaints.

# The attitude

The attitude behind red flags must be positive. A CEO can raise a red flag on their own Trilovation to signal that they have identified a risk, then can then record how they resolved it or what they tried and failed to resolve it.
We DO NOT wait to discuss and resolve a risk and treat a red flag as a last resort. It should be the first step. Write it, seeks responses, and if not resolved engaged in discussions and record conclusions.

Whether a Trilovation is a success or not, the red flags and decisions recorded should tell us the whole high level story of important events.
